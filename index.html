About Signabroam

A home for radio-related projects, online tools, and the services that are still growing today.

Created in 2024 by Discode Studio, Signabroam is a hosting project rooted in the world of radio. It began as a hub for a variety of online services, bringing together tools and projects for listeners, creators, and online communities.

Its original lineup included an online radio player, Discord bots, and SWPedia, a wiki focused on shortwave radio, along with other projects. Over time, many of these services were discontinued, but some are still here—and the story is not over.

The radio player is one example: it is still available at dc.signabroam.ovh, where it has continued to evolve and improve. Today, Signabroam remains a home for the projects that are active, while keeping the spirit of its original radio-focused hub alive.
